Quick guide

Ad Rotator:
a different ad every playlist loop

Put several ads in one playlist slot. Each time your playlist goes round, the next ad plays. No coding, no server, works offline.

How it plays

LOOP 1Your content›Your content›Ad 1
LOOP 2Your content›Your content›Ad 2
LOOP 3Your content›Your content›Ad 3
LOOP 4Your content›Your content›Ad 1↺ starts again

Four steps

1UnzipOpen the ad-rotator.zip download
2Add your adsInto the ads folder, named 01, 02, 03…
3Zip it againSelect the contents, not the folder
4UploadAdd the zip to your playlist

You'll need

  • ad-rotator.zip from github.com/easysignage/Digital-Signage-Ads-Rotation (Releases).
  • Your ads as image or video files. See the list of supported files on page 2.
  • An EasySignage playlist to add it to.

1Unzip the download

Mac: double-click ad-rotator.zip. A folder appears next to it.

Windows: right-click ad-rotator.zip → Extract All… → Extract.

Open the new folder. Inside you'll find:

ads/ ← your ads app/ don't change index.html leave it here HOW-TO-USE.txt quick steps

2Add your ads

Copy your ads into the ads folder and rename each one with its play number. The number decides the order:

ads/ 01.jpg plays on loop 1 02.mp4 plays on loop 2 03.png plays on loop 3
  • Start at 01 and count up with no gaps. If 03 is missing, 04 and later never play.
  • One file per number.
  • You can delete PUT-YOUR-ADS-HERE.txt.

Supported files

TypeFormatsTips
ImagesJPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVGMake them the same size as your screen, for example 1920 × 1080.
VideosMP4, MOV, M4V, WebMExport as H.264 MP4, 1080p, 30 fps. Videos play without sound.
Not supportedHEIC, TIFF, BMP, AVI, WMV, MKVConvert to JPG, PNG or MP4 first.
Keep your ads the same length, for example all 10 or all 15 seconds. The slot has one duration in your playlist.

4Add it to your playlist

  1. In EasySignage, open your playlist and upload the zip like any other file.
  2. Wait until it finishes checking the package.
  3. Set the item's duration to the length of your ads (the longest one if they differ).
  4. Publish to your screens.

That's it. Each time the playlist reaches the Ad Rotator, the next ad plays. Each screen remembers its place, even after a restart.

Changing your ads later

  1. Open your unzipped folder (keep it somewhere safe).
  2. Add, remove or replace files in ads, and keep the numbers in order with no gaps.
  3. Zip the contents again (step 3), and upload the new zip in place of the old one.

A new upload starts the rotation again from ad 01.

If something isn't right

What you seeWhat to do
"index.html not found at the root of the zip file"You zipped the folder. Open it, select the 4 items inside, and zip those (step 3).
"Add your ads to the ads folder, named 01, 02, 03…" on screenThere's no ad numbered 01. Rename your first ad 01.jpg (or 01.png, 01.mp4…).
Some ads never playCheck for a gap in the numbers (01, 02, 04) or an unsupported file type.
A video shows blackExport it again as H.264 MP4. iPhone videos are often HEVC, which some screens can't play.
An ad looks cropped or has barsResize it to your screen size, for example 1920 × 1080.
The same ad plays every timeContact support with your screen type. Some players need an update to remember the next ad.

Good to know

  • Proof of play reports show the Ad Rotator as one item, not each ad.
  • You can use more than one Ad Rotator in a playlist; each keeps its own place.
  • To give one ad more plays, add it twice, for example as 01.jpg and 03.jpg.
