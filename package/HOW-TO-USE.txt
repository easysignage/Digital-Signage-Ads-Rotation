AD ROTATOR - HOW TO USE
=======================

Plays one ad each time your playlist goes round:
loop 1 plays ad 1, loop 2 plays ad 2, and so on, then it starts again at ad 1.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------

  ads/             <- PUT YOUR ADS HERE
  app/             the app itself, no need to open it
  index.html       leave this file where it is
  HOW-TO-USE.txt   this guide


STEP 1 - ADD YOUR ADS
---------------------

Copy your ads into the "ads" folder and rename them by play order:

  01.jpg
  02.mp4
  03.png

- Images: jpg, jpeg, png, webp, gif, svg
- Videos: mp4 (H.264), webm, mov, m4v. They play without sound.
- Number them with no gaps: 01, 02, 03. If 03 is missing, 04 and later never play.
- Only one file per number.
- Give every ad the same duration: make all videos the same length
  (for example all 15 seconds). Images show for the Ad Rotator's duration.
- The leading 0 keeps them in play order in your folder. 1, 2, 3 works too.


STEP 2 - ZIP IT AGAIN
---------------------

Open this folder and select EVERYTHING INSIDE IT (ads, app, index.html,
HOW-TO-USE.txt), then:

  Mac:      right-click > Compress
  Windows:  right-click > Send to > Compressed (zipped) folder

Do NOT zip the folder itself. index.html must be at the top of the zip,
or the upload will say "index.html not found at the root of the zip file".


STEP 3 - ADD IT TO YOUR PLAYLIST
--------------------------------

1. In your playlist, upload the zip like any other file.
2. Wait for it to finish validating.
3. Set its duration to match your ads: the length of your videos, or how
   long each image should show. Example: 15-second videos -> 15 seconds.
4. Publish.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Proof of play reports show the Ad Rotator as one item, not each ad.
- To change the ads, unzip your copy, swap the files in "ads", zip it again
  and upload it in place of the old one.
- Using two Ad Rotators in one playlist is fine; each keeps its own place.
